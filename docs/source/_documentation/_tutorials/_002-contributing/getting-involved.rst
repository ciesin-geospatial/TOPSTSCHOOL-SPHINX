We're thrilled you're interested in being part of the TOPSTSCHOOL community!

We believe in the power of collaboration as an important practice of 
:term:`open science` in research. Whether you’re a Subject Matter Expert (SME), 
an open science enthusiast, or a newcomer excited to contribute, this guide will 
help you find your place in the movement.

From what we see, every contributor plays a vital role in our
mission to create tools, tutorials, and resources that break down barriers to
scientific knowledge. Your enthusiasm, whether you're new to contributing or a
seasoned professional, makes this community vibrant and impactful.
 

.. _why-contribute:

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Why Contribute?
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The |website-tops| Training initiative  advocates for open science, 
a transformative approach to research that emphasizes transparency, 
inclusivity, and collaboration. By contributing you're helping to:

- **Empower researchers, scientists and educators** by providing clear,
  accessible resources.
- **Break down barriers to knowledge** by creating tools and lessons that are
  openly available.
- **Foster a global community of learners** dedicated to science and
  innovation.



.. _ways-to-contribute:



-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Ways to Contribute
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

.. _become-sme:

**Become a Subject Matter Expert (SME)**  
Are you an expert in climate, water, disasters, air quality, agriculture, or environmental justice? Join our advisory team and:

- Provide expert input for thematic learning modules.
- Participate in hybrid workshops and sprints.
- Promote Open Science values through your guidance.

.. _sme google form: https://forms.gle/USbE8itQpQ4A67AM7  
- :email: TOPSTSCHOOL@gmail.com

.. _join-open-science-team:

**Join the Open Science Team**  
Collaborate with educators and researchers to build FAIR, ethical learning tools. As a team member, you'll:

- Join collaborative workshops and content development sprints.
- Offer feedback and improve module accessibility.
- Champion inclusivity in pedagogy and design.

.. _participant google form: https://forms.gle/U8ZEfbE14uhBjTZDA
- :email: TOPSTSCHOOL@gmail.com

.. _how-to-contribute:

**Contribute to the Documentation**  
Ready to roll up your sleeves? Our documentation and learning resources are all open and maintained by contributors like you.

Here’s how to get started:

- **Explore the repository and structure.**  
  Understand where files live and what they do.
- **Learn the tools.**  
  We use `Sphinx` and `reStructuredText (rST)` for documentation. They're beginner-friendly and powerful.
- **Make your first contribution.**  
  Whether it’s fixing a typo, improving a guide, or adding a new section, your effort counts.



-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Let’s Build the Future of Open Science Together
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

TOPSTSCHOOL is more than a platform—it’s a community-driven movement. With your help, we can make science education:

- **Accessible** to everyone, everywhere.
- **Collaborative** and continuously improving.
- **Transparent** and rooted in ethical, inclusive practice.

Your contributions, no matter the size, ripple outward, touching the lives of
people across the globe and paving the way for transformative scientific
discoveries. Every effort you make is a part of something larger than all of
us, and we are genuinely grateful to have you on this journey with us.
